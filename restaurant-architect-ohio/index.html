What does JKA design for a restaurant?

The whole room and how it works: the entry and host stand, the dining room and its seat count, the bar, the kitchen and its flow from delivery to dish return, restrooms, storage and the patio. The design sets the mood with light, material and furniture, and it fits the kitchen equipment, hood and plumbing the menu needs.

Which approvals does a restaurant need in Ohio?

Two reviews before construction, and more depending on the menu and the building:

  • The health department. Plans for a new or remodeled food service operation go to the local health department for review and approval before construction begins, under the Ohio Uniform Food Safety Code. JKA draws the layout, equipment plan and finish schedule that review asks for.
  • The building department. Sealed drawings go through plan approval under the Ohio Building Code, with the kitchen hood, grease interceptor and plumbing drawn by engineers JKA coordinates.
  • Zoning, when the space was not a restaurant before, and a liquor permit from the Ohio Division of Liquor Control if the restaurant will serve alcohol (applied for by the owner).

Does the number of seats matter?

Yes. Under the Ohio Building Code, a restaurant with an occupant load of fewer than 50 people is classed as a business occupancy; at 50 or more it becomes an assembly occupancy, which brings more exits and can bring sprinklers. Seat count also drives the number of restrooms. JKA works the occupant load out early so the menu, the seats and the budget agree.

How does a restaurant job run?

Send the address, the size of the space, the concept and the opening date through the inquiry form. JKA reviews the space and the lease, measures, and agrees the scope and fee in writing. A layout and seat count come first, then the design, then the permit and health department sets. JKA answers reviewers' comments and the contractor's questions through construction.

Short answers

Do I need an architect to open a restaurant in Ohio?

Usually, yes. Restaurants fall under the Ohio Building Code, and plans for that work generally need the seal of an Ohio registered architect or engineer before the building department will approve them.

Does the health department review restaurant plans in Ohio?

Yes. Under the Ohio Uniform Food Safety Code, plans for a new or remodeled food service operation are reviewed and approved by the local health department before construction begins.

Can a former shop become a restaurant?

Often, but it is a change of use. The zoning must allow a restaurant, and the building may need new exits, restrooms, a kitchen hood, a grease interceptor or sprinklers. A review before signing the lease shows what it will take.
