Tenant improvement drawings in Ohio

James Kehl Architects draws tenant improvements for businesses and landlords anywhere in Ohio: an office, salon, studio, clinic or shop fitted out in leased space. The drawings are prepared, signed and sealed by James Kehl, RA, an Ohio registered architect, ready for the building department, drawn quickly and on a fixed fee agreed in the proposal before work starts.

What is a tenant improvement?

The work done inside a leased commercial space to fit it to the business that will use it: new walls, doors, lighting and ceilings, restrooms, finishes, and changes to heating, cooling, plumbing and power. The building department treats it as an alteration under the Ohio Building Code and the 2024 Ohio Existing Building Code, so it needs plan approval and a permit before work starts.

Who is it for?

Business owners opening or moving into a space; landlords preparing a vacant suite or a white box for a new tenant; and brokers who want a quick test fit to show what a space can hold. Typical jobs are offices, salons and barbers, fitness and yoga studios, small medical and dental suites, showrooms and shops.

What is in the drawing set?

  • existing conditions and demolition plans, from a site visit to measure;
  • a code plan: occupancy, occupant load, exit paths and the accessible route;
  • the new floor plan with furniture and equipment, and a reflected ceiling plan;
  • finish plans and schedules, door and hardware schedules, interior elevations and details;
  • engineering drawings for mechanical, electrical and plumbing work, from Ohio registered engineers JKA brings in and coordinates when the job needs them.

How long does it take?

The drawing time is set in the proposal, and JKA works fast. The building department's review time depends on the city or county; JKA answers plan review comments quickly so the permit is not held up on the design side.

What should the client have ready?

The lease or the landlord's tenant criteria, any drawings of the building or the suite, the number of staff and visitors expected, and a list of rooms and equipment. If no drawings exist, JKA measures the space.

Short answers

Do I need an architect for a tenant improvement in Ohio?

In most cases, yes. Ohio Revised Code 3791.04 requires an architect's or engineer's seal on plans for buildings under the Ohio Building Code when the work involves technical design analysis, such as new exits, restrooms, walls or systems.

Does painting or new carpet need sealed drawings?

Usually not. Work that is cosmetic or replaces like with like generally does not involve technical design analysis, but check with the local building department before starting.

Can a landlord hire JKA to prepare a space for leasing?

Yes. JKA draws white box and spec suite improvements for landlords and quick test fits that show a prospective tenant how a space could work.
