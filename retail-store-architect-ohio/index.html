Retail stores and rollouts in Ohio

James Kehl Architects designs retail stores anywhere in Ohio: a single shop for an independent owner, or a store set adapted site by site for a brand opening several locations. Each set is prepared, signed and sealed by James Kehl, RA, an Ohio registered architect, drawn quickly and on a fixed fee agreed in the proposal before work starts.

What does JKA draw for a store?

The sales floor and fixture plan, the fitting rooms, cash wrap and stockroom, the storefront and sign location, lighting and ceilings, finishes, restrooms and the accessible route. The permit set is coordinated with the engineers' mechanical, electrical and plumbing drawings and with the landlord's tenant criteria.

How do rollouts for brands work?

A brand opening several stores in Ohio usually has a prototype: a standard plan, fixtures and finishes. JKA adapts it to each site, measures the space, checks the landlord's criteria and the local code, and seals the drawings as the Ohio architect of record, so the brand gets the same store every time and each building department gets a set drawn for that address. Each location starts with a short proposal and a fixed fee.

Why use an Ohio architect for a chain store?

Ohio requires the seal of an Ohio registered architect or engineer on plans for this work (Ohio Revised Code 3791.04), and an Ohio architect may seal only drawings prepared under his or her own responsible charge. A local architect also knows the building departments and their review practice, and can visit the site.

What should the client have ready?

For an owner: the lease, any drawings of the space, and a list of what the store sells and how. For a brand: the prototype drawings and fixture package, the landlord's tenant criteria for each site, and the opening dates.

Serving shop owners and brands across Ohio, including Central Ohio, Columbus suburbs, Northeast Ohio, Eastern Ohio and the Ohio Valley, Southwest Ohio, Northwest Ohio and Southeast Ohio. See also tenant improvements and restaurants.

Short answers

Can an Ohio architect seal a brand's standard store drawings?

Only if the drawings are prepared under that architect's responsible charge. JKA adapts the prototype to each site and prepares the Ohio set, so it can seal it as architect of record.

Does a retail store need a permit in Ohio?

A store build-out that changes walls, exits, restrooms or building systems needs plan approval and a building permit under the Ohio Building Code before work starts.

Can JKA work on stores across the state at once?

Yes. JKA works anywhere in Ohio, remotely first, with a site visit to measure each space.
